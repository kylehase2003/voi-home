# Voi Home — Voice

Voi Home is a personal, boutique real estate firm in Türkiye. We are selective about the
homes we represent and attentive to every client, each known by name. The writing should
feel like that: unhurried, attentive, and specific.

**The image to write toward: fine wine.** Layered rather than loud. It rewards a
second read. It names the vineyard, the year, the slope — never just "excellent."

## Principles

1. **Specific over superlative.** Say what the light does in the afternoon, not
   "stunning views." Say "title deed, zoning, the developer's last three handovers,"
   not "full due diligence." If a sentence would fit any agency's website, rewrite it.
2. **Patient, not slow.** Sentences can breathe — a longer line followed by a short
   one — but every clause earns its place. No filler, no throat-clearing.
3. **Personal, never familiar.** "We" and "you," one advisor to one client. Warm, but
   with the manners of someone you'd trust with a large decision. No slang, no
   exclamation marks, no emoji.
4. **Quiet confidence.** No hype words: *luxury, premium, world-class, dream home,
   unparalleled, exclusive deals, don't miss out*. Quality is shown through detail,
   never claimed.
5. **Sensory where it matters, precise where it counts.** Homes get texture — stone,
   light, the sound of a street. Money, law, and process get plain, exact language.
   Never be poetic about a contract.
6. **Chosen, not collected.** We talk about homes we have selected, not a catalogue.
   Avoid volume language ("thousands of listings," "huge selection").
7. **Never call Voi Home "small."** Boutique and selective describe how we choose homes
   and treat clients, not the size of the team or of the properties. Avoid
   "small house" and "real estate house" (ambiguous in real estate); say "firm".

## Mechanics

- Headlines: sentence case, usually a full thought, often ending in a period.
- Buttons: plain verbs, two to four words ("View the home," "Speak with us").
- Labels, filters, form fields: plain and functional — the voice lives around them,
  not in them. "Bedrooms," not "Places to rest."
- Numbers: exact where we have them. Currency with symbol, thousands separated.
- Place names: Türkiye, İstanbul in Turkish contexts, Istanbul in English prose is fine.
- Dashboard (admin) copy stays clear and utilitarian; consistency beats flourish.

## Audiences

We write for four readers at once, without splitting the voice: the foreign investor
reading numbers from abroad, the family choosing where to live, the citizenship
applicant who needs the process to be exact, and the local buyer who knows the
city better than any brochure. Specific, honest writing serves all four.

## Arabic and Russian

Translate the meaning and the register, not the words. Arabic should read as refined
Modern Standard Arabic; Russian as polished, formal-but-warm (use "Вы").
